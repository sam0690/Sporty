sporty

The Convergence Mark — one league, every game.

The idea

Distinct sports, resolved to one point.

The mark is a single geometric S whose spine passes exactly through a central node — the point where football, basketball and cricket converge into one squad. Read a second way, that node is a broadcast aperture: the moment the match comes into focus and points move live. It is the one thing no competitor's mark can claim — fusion — drawn in champagne gold on broadcast ink.

Convergence

Three sports, one lineup. The strokes of the S lead the eye into a single meeting point.

Live focus

The node reads as a lens aperture — the real-time heartbeat of match-driven scoring.

Earned status

Gold on ink: premium and warm, never sportsbook-loud, never fintech-cold.

Construction

Built on a point-symmetric grid.

Canvas96 × 96 units
Optical extentsx 30–66 · y 18–78
Stroke weight12 u · round caps
Convergence noder = 10 u @ (48, 48)
Node : stroke ratio1.67 ×
Symmetry180° rotational about centre

The spine's midpoint lands on exactly (48, 48), so the node is never approximate — it sits on the true inflection. Rotate the mark 180° and it is identical. Never redraw by hand; scale the vector.

Core variants

One mark, four registers.

Primary · gold on ink
Mono · white on ink
Light · ink on white
Champion · title moments

On white, bright gold fails contrast — switch to ink or --accent-dim #b39a55. The champion gradient is reserved for trophy lifts and season awards, never the default UI.

Contextual skins

The master stays gold. Context flexes.

In-app, a sport's page may tint the icon with its reserved neon token — never the master mark, never marketing. The geometry never changes; only the single colour does.

Motion

It resolves — then it pulses on the play.

Two states only. On load, three sport strokes slide into the node and settle to gold (below). Live, on a goal / basket / wicket, the node gives a single gold pulse. Motion is the exception, not the ambience.
